05 / About page evolution

About ISG,
reframed with clarity.

The original About Us page was functional but text-heavy and visually limited. The redesign turns that same story into a richer brand page with a stronger introduction, clearer value messaging, a visual timeline, and a more human leadership section.

BeforeOriginal About Us page
Legacy page↗ expand

The older page relied on a very basic content column, minimal imagery, and long uninterrupted text blocks that made the story feel flat and less engaging.

Stronger first impression

A clearer headline, sharper imagery, and a more confident layout immediately communicate who ISG is and what makes the cooperative valuable.

Better storytelling

Mission, value, history, and leadership are broken into purposeful sections so the page reads more like a guided brand story than a single wall of copy.

More credibility

Timeline moments, stats, and leadership portraits help make the organization feel established, transparent, and member-focused.

06 / Conversion-focused redesign

Join Us,
made more inviting.

The old Join ISG page centered on a basic form and dense explanatory copy. The redesign opens with a stronger introduction, gives visitors clearer paths for becoming a member or supplier, and makes the page feel like an intentional entry point into the network.

BeforeOriginal Join ISG page
Form-heavy entry point↗ expand

The previous version asked visitors to commit to a long form right away and offered little visual guidance or structure around the two audiences the page serves.

AfterRedesigned Join Us experience
Clear member & supplier paths↗ expand

The redesign shifts from a single generic form to a guided experience with stronger hierarchy, two distinct pathways, benefit highlights, and a friendlier call to action.

From form page to growth path.

The updated page is designed to convert by helping users understand where they fit in the ISG ecosystem before asking them to take action. It balances explanation, credibility, and next-step clarity—especially for members and suppliers who arrive with different goals.
